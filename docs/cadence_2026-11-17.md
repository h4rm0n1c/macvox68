# Mac speech mapping: internal boundaries, November 17

The listener rejected the November 16 sample because its pauses were excessive.
The reference preprocessing remains unchanged; the Mac rendering is deliberately
different from FlexTalk's sentence-terminating br behavior.

Internal br now preserves a word boundary without injecting punctuation or a
silence command. Existing sentence punctuation survives, and sf retains the
bounded explicit final silence. This does not yet recreate FlexTalk aspiration,
intonation or word timing. In particular, sentence-end punctuation plus sf500
still gives a substantial sentence pause.

Two rejected guest experiments informed this choice: comma boundaries produced
an 8.66-second active span, and requested 60 ms silences produced 7.28 seconds.
Apple's embedded-command documentation (corpus 23623, Inside Macintosh: Sound,
Writing Embedded Speech Commands) explicitly says silence timing varies widely
between synthesizers. A requested delay is not a measured acoustic delay.

The final MacVox-lightbeats4 sample, “A reminder to all personnel. This tram is
departing.”, has a 5.76-second active span. A same-session old-build control had
5.44 seconds. These spans do not demonstrate a speed improvement: removing
artificial sentence endings also changes pronunciation and phrasing. Listening
is the acceptance check. The new recording was played directly through
plughw:CARD=ATR2USB,DEV=0, with only leading/trailing silence trimmed for playback.

Lab-local evidence: .mac-lab/lightbeats-audio-proof.json, lightbeats-0.wav,
lightbeats-listen.wav, lightbeats-timed-proof.json and lightbeats-timed.wav.
The comma trial's original WAV was overwritten during the subsequent capture;
its measured span is recorded here, not presented as reproducible WAV evidence.
The browser viewer and live USB forwarding stayed off during capture.

Validation: parser ASan/UBSan checks and host CTest pass; the reference comparison
still matches both external implementations on 68/68 ASCII cases. Existing fake
Open Transport and speech ownership contracts pass. Production/instrumented
Retro68 builds succeed. Regression fixtures cover internal word boundaries,
source period/exclamation preservation, and single final-silence emission.

The final staged build also passed interpreter CPU-trap checks of SpeakText's
big-endian arguments, exact generated buffer, return value, independent speech
buffer ownership, repeated cancellation, natural completion and active-Quit
disposal (.mac-lab/lightbeats-trap.log).
